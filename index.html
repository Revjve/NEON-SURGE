THE RUN

  • Each wave is timed. Survive until the timer hits zero and the arena is purged.
  • After every wave, pick 1 of 3 upgrades. They stack: extra bolts carry every bullet mod, so Split Barrel + Volatile Rounds means every bolt explodes.
  • Every 5th wave brings an elite. The wave doesn't end until it's destroyed.
  • Kills drop gold flux (score multiplier) and charge SURGE, a screen-wiping shockwave.
  • When your hull breaks, the run's Neon Shards go to the Hangar for permanent upgrades.

HOSTILES

  • DARTRelentless chaser. Hunts in packs.
  • WISPWeaves and side-steps your fire.
  • LANCERLocks on, then dashes along its beam.
  • BULWARKSlow armoured tank. Huge explosion.
  • HIVEBursts into fast mites when destroyed.
  • SENTRYKeeps its distance and fires plasma orbs. Shoot the orbs down.
  • ELITECrowned, armoured variant of a heavy. Pays a shard bounty.
